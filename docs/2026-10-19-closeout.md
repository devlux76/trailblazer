## Defect found during independent verification

The baseline suite failed two Query tests under seed `378724`: each expected no
armed backstops after work finished but observed one. Inspection showed the
global EmbedHealth process was down with no waiting consumers, no retry timer
and no active probe, while still holding an `:embedder_down` backstop.

MiniMax M3.1 Flash implemented a bounded two-file repair, and Codex reviewed and
integrated it. When the last consumer leaves, EmbedHealth cancels its retry,
disarms the outage and probe guards, and terminates any abandoned probe. The
state remains down until recovery is actually observed. A new period of demand
arms a new outage guard; joining consumers cannot rearm a guard that already
fired. Stale or duplicate probe messages cannot create an unwanted worker or
replace the active one. Published down reasons retain their inspected-string
format; internal reasons retain their original terms.

The new regressions failed against the previous implementation, and the review
also caught and corrected rearming and queued-message defects in the first
proposed patch. Tests use monitor/event synchronization. The spent-guard test
injects the existing owner notification after disarming the real guard; it tests
the health handler's post-fire behavior without depending on a 1 ms race. It
does not add coverage of Backstop's unchanged timer-to-notification mechanism.
